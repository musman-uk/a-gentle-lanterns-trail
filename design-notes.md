# Design Notes

These notes capture the early thinking behind *Lanterns Trail*.  
They are not final – they are sparks, motions, and fragments that may shift or fade as the project grows.

## Themes

- movement as expression  
- light as guidance  
- the interplay between motion and illumination  
- quiet discovery through traversal  
- the emotional rhythm of wandering  
- the softness of being led by light  

The game should feel like following a gentle path, where light responds to movement and movement responds to light.

## Core Scope

The first version of *Lanterns Trail* is intentionally small.  
One path, one lantern, one relationship.

- light reacts subtly to the player’s movement  
- motion shapes the brightness, colour, or reach of the lantern  
- the player interacts by moving with intention and noticing how the world responds  

This limited scope keeps the project focused and allows the relationship between motion and light to lead the design.

## Early Mechanics (Exploratory)

- a lantern whose glow shifts with speed, direction, or proximity  
- trails of light that linger briefly as the player moves  
- soft audio cues tied to footsteps, illumination, or stillness  
- environmental elements that brighten or dim in response to motion  
- a sense of quiet reward in discovering how movement shapes the world  

Nothing here is complex. The mechanics should feel like breath – gentle, responsive, and unforced.

## Future Additions (Possibilities)

These are ideas for later, once the core is stable:

- multiple lantern types with different behaviours  
- small environmental puzzles shaped by light and motion  
- subtle colour shifts that reflect the player’s path or choices  
- ambient creatures or particles that follow or avoid the lantern  
- a sense of companionship formed through the lantern’s evolving behaviour  

These are not promises – only trails the project may wander toward.

## Tone & Interaction

- no urgency, no failure states  
- movement should feel expressive, not pressured  
- the lantern is a companion, not a tool  
- the world should feel soft, reactive, and quietly alive  

The design will emerge gradually, shaped by what feels true to the project’s tone.
